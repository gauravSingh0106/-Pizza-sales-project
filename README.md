 ## Project Overview
This project focuses on analyzing sales performance for a pizza place throughout 2015.<br>
The main objective was to clean raw sales data, model it effectively,<br>
and build an interactive Power BI dashboard to track overall revenue, order volume,<br>
peak sales periods, and top-selling pizza categories/sizes. <br>
This report helps management identify consumer preferences and optimize stock and staffing according to demand trends.<br>


### Business Questions Addressed & Insights <br>

**1. Which days generate the highest sales volume?** <br>
Friday is the busiest day of the week with 8,106 orders, followed closely by Thursday (7,323 orders). Sundays see the lowest order count (5,917 orders). <br><br>

**2. What are the peak sales months during the year?** <br>
July saw the highest order volume (4,301 orders), whereas October registered the lowest (3,797 orders). <br><br>

**3. Which pizza size generates the most revenue?** <br>
Large (L) size pizzas dominate sales, contributing 45.8% of total revenue. Medium (M) size accounts for 30.49%, and Small (S) size makes up 21.77%. <br><br>

**4. How are orders distributed across pizza categories?** <br>
The order split is almost evenly distributed: Classic (30%), Supreme (24%), Veggie (24%), and Chicken (22%). <br><br>

**5. Which category brings in the highest revenue?** <br>
Classic category generated the highest total revenue (₹220,053) with 14,888 pizzas sold, followed by Supreme (₹208,197) and Chicken (₹195,920).
