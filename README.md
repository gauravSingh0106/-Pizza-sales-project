Excited to share one of my latest Power BI Dashboard projects, where I analyzed a full year of pizza sales data (Jan 2015 – Dec 2015) to uncover meaningful business insights and support data-driven decision making.

📊 Dashboard Highlights
✅ Total Revenue: ₹8,17,860
 ✅ Total Orders: 48,620
 ✅ Total Quantity Sold: 49,574
 ✅ Average Order Value: ₹17
🔍 Key Business Insights
📌 1. Weekend Sales Dominate

Friday (8,106 orders) recorded the highest number of orders.
Saturday (7,355 orders) was the second busiest day.
This indicates customers prefer ordering pizza before and during weekends, making these ideal days for promotional campaigns and higher inventory planning.


📌 2. Classic Pizza is the Revenue Leader
The Classic category generated nearly 30% of total revenue, making it the strongest-performing category.
It should remain the primary focus for marketing and combo offers.


📌 3. Medium Size is the Customer Favorite
Medium (M) pizzas contributed approximately 46% of total revenue, outperforming all other sizes.
This suggests customers perceive Medium pizzas as offering the best value for money.


📌 4. Monthly Demand Fluctuation
July recorded the highest monthly orders (4,301).
October showed the lowest performance (3,797).
Understanding seasonal demand helps businesses optimize staffing and inventory.

📌 5. Category-wise Performance
Classic Pizza generated the highest revenue.
Chicken Pizza received comparatively fewer orders.
This opens opportunities for targeted promotions to improve underperforming categories.

📌 6. Price Analysis
Maximum Pizza Price: ₹83
Minimum Pizza Price: ₹9.75
Average Pizza Price: ₹16.82
This pricing range indicates the business serves customers across multiple budget segments.
