## 🚀 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing sales performance for a pizza place throughout 2015. The objective was to clean raw sales data, model it effectively, and build an interactive Power BI dashboard tracking overall revenue, order volume, peak sales periods, and top-selling pizza categories/sizes — helping management identify consumer preferences and optimize stock and staffing according to demand trends.

## ❓ Business Questions Addressed & Insights

**1. Which days generate the highest sales volume?**
Friday is the busiest day of the week with 8,106 orders, followed closely by Thursday (7,323 orders). Sundays see the lowest order count (5,917 orders).

**2. What are the peak sales months during the year?**
July saw the highest order volume (4,301 orders), while October registered the lowest (3,797 orders).

**3. Which pizza size generates the most revenue?**
Large (L) pizzas dominate sales, contributing 45.8% of total revenue. Medium (M) accounts for 30.49%, and Small (S) makes up 21.77%.

**4. How are orders distributed across pizza categories?**
The order split is nearly even: Classic (30%), Supreme (24%), Veggie (24%), and Chicken (22%).

**5. Which category brings in the highest revenue?**
Classic generated the highest total revenue (₹2,20,053) from 14,888 pizzas sold, followed by Supreme (₹2,08,197) and Chicken (₹1,95,920).
